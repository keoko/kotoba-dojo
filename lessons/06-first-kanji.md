# Lesson 6 — Your first kanji

> **Goal:** understand how kanji work and learn your first ~30 kanji, ones you already "know" from the dojo (lesson 8 brings you to ~40).

## 1. What a kanji is

A kanji is a character that carries **meaning** and usually has **two kinds of readings**:

- **On'yomi** (音読み) — "sound reading", borrowed from Chinese. Used mostly in compounds. Often short: *dō*, *ki*, *sei*.
- **Kun'yomi** (訓読み) — "meaning reading", the native Japanese word. Used when the kanji stands alone or with hiragana endings: *michi*, *te*, *ue*.

Example: **道** (way)
- on: **dō** → 合気**道** *aiki**dō***, **道**場 ***dō**jō*
- kun: **michi** → 道 *michi* "road, path"

You don't need to memorise every reading. Learn kanji **through words**, and the readings come with them.

## 2. The name of your art: 合気道

| Kanji | Reading here | Meaning | Other use |
|-------|-------------|---------|-----------|
| **合** | ai | join, fit, match | 合う *au* — to fit, to meet |
| **気** | ki | spirit, energy, mind | 元気 *genki* — healthy, energetic |
| **道** | dō | way, path | 道 *michi* — road |

**合気道** = "the way of harmonising energy". And now you see why **気合** *kiai* (the shout) is the same two kanji reversed: "energy joined".

## 3. Dojo kanji

| Word | Kanji | Literal |
|------|-------|---------|
| dōjō | **道場** | way + place |
| sensei | **先生** | before + born |
| rei | **礼** | courtesy, bow |
| seiza | **正座** | correct + sitting |
| keiko | **稽古** | thinking about + the old |
| budō | **武道** | martial + way |
| waza | **技** | technique, skill |
| uke | **受け** | receive (+ hiragana け) |
| nage | **投げ** | throw (+ hiragana げ) |
| ukemi | **受身** | receive + body |

Notice **受け** and **投げ**: kanji for the meaning, hiragana for the ending. That hiragana tail is called **okurigana**. It's where verbs change: 投げ**る** *nageru*, 投げ**ます** *nagemasu*.

## 4. Number kanji

The easiest kanji — the first three are literally lines.

| 一 | 二 | 三 | 四 | 五 | 六 | 七 | 八 | 九 | 十 |
|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| ichi | ni | san | shi / yon | go | roku | shichi / nana | hachi | ku / kyū | jū |

So:

| Technique | Kanji |
|-----------|-------|
| ikkyō | **一教** |
| nikyō | **二教** |

| shihō-nage | **四方投げ** — four + direction + throw |
| jūji-nage | **十字投げ** — ten + character + throw |

## 7. How to study kanji

1. Learn the **meaning first**, one reading second.
2. Write each kanji 10 times, saying the word aloud.
3. Stroke order matters (top → bottom, left → right, horizontal before vertical). Look up animations at [jisho.org](https://jisho.org) — search a kanji and click "stroke order".
4. Review the [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) weekly.

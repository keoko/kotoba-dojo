# Lesson 8 — Concepts and a full class

> **Goal:** read the key concepts of aikido in kanji, and follow a whole class in Japanese.

## 1. The 気 family

Once you know **気** (*ki*), you see it everywhere in Japanese:

| Word | Kanji | Literal | Meaning |
|------|-------|---------|---------|
| **aiki** | 合気 | joined energy | harmony of energy |
| **kiai** | 気合 | energy joined | focused shout / spirit |
| **genki** | 元気 | origin energy | healthy, energetic — *O-genki desu ka?* "How are you?" |
| **tenki** | 天気 | heaven energy | weather |
| **byōki** | 病気 | sick energy | illness |
| **ki o tsukete** | 気をつけて | attach energy | take care! |

Same character, everyday words. This is how kanji pay you back: learn one, read ten.

## 2. Core concepts

| Romaji | Kanji | Literal | Meaning in aikido |
|--------|-------|---------|-------------------|
| **zanshin** | 残心 | remaining mind | Staying aware after the technique |
| **ma-ai** | 間合い | interval + meeting | The right distance between partners |
| **kokyū** | 呼吸 | exhale + inhale | Breath, breath power |
| **kokyū-hō** | 呼吸法 | breath method | Breath-power exercise (often seated) |
| **musubi** | 結び | tying | Connection with the partner |
| **hanmi** | 半身 | half body | The triangular stance |
| **shikkō** | 膝行 | knee + go | Knee walking |
| **kamiza** | 上座 | upper seat | The front, place of honour |
| **kaiso** | 開祖 | open + ancestor | The founder (Ueshiba Morihei, 植芝盛平) |
| **shoshin** | 初心 | first mind | Beginner's mind |
| **misogi** | 禊 | purification | Purification practice |
| **takemusu aiki** | 武産合気 | martial + birth + aiki | Spontaneous, creative aikido |

Notice **心** (*shin*, "heart/mind") in *zanshin* and *shoshin*, and **身** (*mi/shin*, "body") in *hanmi*. They sound the same in compounds — *shin* — but the kanji tells you whether it's mind or body. This is why kanji matter in Japanese: many words share sounds.

## 3. Ranks and the dojo

| Romaji | Kanji | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **kyū** | 級 | grade (below black belt; counts *down*: 6th kyū → 1st kyū) |
| **dan** | 段 | step (black belt; counts *up*: 1st dan → …) |
| **shodan** | 初段 | first dan (初 = first, beginning) |
| **sempai / senpai** | 先輩 | senior student (先 = before, as in 先生) |
| **kōhai** | 後輩 | junior student (後 = after) |
| **shihan** | 師範 | master instructor |
| **dōgi** | 道着 | training uniform (way + wear) |
| **hakama** | 袴 | pleated trousers |
| **obi** | 帯 | belt |
| **bokken** | 木剣 | wooden sword (tree + sword) |
| **jō** | 杖 | staff |
| **tantō** | 短刀 | knife (short + blade) |

Look: **先** appears in *sensei* (先生, born before) and *senpai* (先輩, colleague before). **初** appears in *shodan* and *shoshin*. Connections everywhere.

## 4. Read a whole class

Here's a class written as Japanese would write it. Try reading it before looking at the translation.

> **先生**：もくそう。…… もくそう、やめ。
> **先生**：しょうめんに、れい。
> **先生**：おたがいに、れい。
> **みんな**：おねがいします！
>

>
> **先生**：みてください。かたてどり、しほうなげ、おもて と うら。
> **先生**：はい、はじめ！
> …
> **先生**：やめ！ こうたい してください。

> …
> **先生**：せいざ。しょうめんに、れい。おたがいに、れい。
> **みんな**：ありがとうございました！

## 5. The same class in normal Japanese writing

Real Japanese mixes kanji and kana. With what you know, you can now follow most of it:

> 先生：正座。黙想。…… 黙想、止め。
> 先生：正面に、礼。お互いに、礼。
> 皆：お願いします！
> 先生：見てください。片手取り、四方投げ、表と裏。はい、始め！
> 先生：止め！ 交代してください。もう一度。
> 皆：ありがとうございました！

## 6. Where to go next

You've finished the course. You can now:
- Read all of hiragana
- Recognise ~40 kanji
- Understand *-masu*, *-mashita*, *-te kudasai*, *-mashō*, and the particles に, の, を, と
- Explain the literal meaning of almost every technique name

Next steps:
1. **Katakana** — the second phonetic script (same sounds as hiragana, different shapes). Two weeks of daily practice.
2. **A beginner textbook** — *Genki I* or *Minna no Nihongo I*. The first chapters will feel like review.
3. **Spaced repetition** — put the [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) into Anki.
4. **Read about aikido in Japanese** — the Aikikai's Japanese pages, or O-Sensei's *dōka* (poems) with a dictionary.
